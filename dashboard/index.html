How the Data-Backed Model Achieves the 25% Reduction with Zero Headcount:

  • Transient Congestion Root Cause: In the baseline model, 35 staff were evenly distributed across 12 hours (~2.9 staff/hr). During the 10:00 - 15:00 surge, incoming volume (7.8 tickets/hr) outpaced capacity (4.1 tickets/hr), creating an accumulating backlog that cascaded across days.
  • Staggered Shift Reallocation: Without hiring any additional headcount, staff schedules were redesigned into overlapping shifts (Early 08:00-16:30, Peak Booster Squad 09:30-18:00, Close 11:30-20:00), concentrating 5.25 staff/hr during peak hours.
  • The Mathematical Result: Queue wait time dropped from 9.6h to 4.0h during peak (-58%), directly driving the verified 25.4% reduction in overall onboarding workflow turnaround time.
